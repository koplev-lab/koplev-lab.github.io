# Editing the lab website

You edit the site in **Pages CMS**, a web app with forms for each part of the site. You don't need
to install anything or know git.

## Signing in

Open <https://app.pagescms.org> and sign in, either with your GitHub account or with the email
link from your invitation. Choose **koplev-lab.github.io**. The menu on the left lists everything
you can edit.

<!-- Screenshot: the Pages CMS menu for this site -->

## What happens when you save

Every **Save** publishes the change. The site rebuilds by itself and is live at
<https://koplev-lab.github.io> about **two minutes** later. Each save is kept in the site's
history, so nothing is ever lost and any change can be undone.

If the site doesn't update, a field may not have passed the site's checks (for example a photo
without a description). Ask Mo; he can see what went wrong.

## Common tasks

### Add a lab member

**People** → **Add an entry**. Fill in name, role and order (lower numbers come first within a
role), and write a short bio: 3–4 sentences, academic background then research interests. Add a
photo if you have one (square works best) and a one-line photo description, e.g. "Yishan Hu".
Without a photo the site shows the two-circle placeholder.

### Update a bio or swap a photo

**People** → click the person → edit → **Save**. To change the photo, click it and upload a new
one; remember to update the description.

### Someone leaves (alumni)

Open the person in **People**, switch off **Current member**, keep **Role** as the position they
held in the lab, and fill in **Next position** once it is confirmed, e.g. "PhD student, ETH
Zurich". Leave it empty until then. Never mention ongoing applications.

### Advertise a position

**Job ads** → **Add an entry**: the title as advertised, the link to the ad (SciLifeLab, KTH or
Varbi page), the last day to apply and one or two plain sentences. It appears on the Join page
and in a "We are hiring" band on the home page, and disappears by itself the day after the
deadline. Old ads can stay in the list; they are simply not shown.

### Feature a publication

1. The paper must be in the publications list (Mo refreshes it from OpenAlex).
2. **Selected publications** → **Add an entry**: the DOI, a figure (upload it), a description of
   the figure, the full credit (e.g. "Koplev et al., Nat. Immunol. 27, 1954–1968 (2026), Fig. 1c,
   cropped") and its licence, and one or two plain sentences on the finding.
3. To show it on the home page: **Page: Home** → **Featured publications**. Three work best.

An entry whose file name starts with `_` is kept off the site (used while a figure is missing).
Rename it without the `_` to publish it.

### Add a group photo

**Page: People** → **Group photos** → add: upload the photo, describe who is in it and where,
and add a short caption. Photos appear at the top of the People page in the order listed.

### Change page text

**Page: Home / Research / About / Join us** hold each page's text. The tagline and description
are in **Lab details and funders**, together with affiliations, funders and the profile links in
the footer. Menu labels and small bits of wording are under **Interface wording**.

### Research projects

**Research projects** has one entry per line of work: title, one-sentence summary, a figure with
caption, short source and full credit, the people involved and related papers (by DOI), then the
description under the headings Question, Approach and Why it matters.

## "Awaiting Simon's approval"

Most entries have this switch. It is not shown on the site; it only marks drafts that Simon
hasn't approved yet. Switch it off once the text is right.

## Figure credits

Captions show only a short source. The full credit and licence of every reused figure is listed
under **Figure credits** on the About page, which is what the figures' licences require. Fill in
both the short source and the full credit when you add a figure.
