We had the opportunity to try the local Swedish "delicacy" also known as surströmming in a safe outdoor environment and scenery, tucked away and conveniently out of sight behind the SciLifeLab buildings. The can opening went according to plan, in contrary to expectations, but the Swedish fermented herring did indeed have strong notes (and several hours of aftertaste) of, curiously, both rancid fish and really old cheese flavours. Mmm. Although Bara here indicates the thumbs up verdict, reviews were mixed, and several fundamental shortcomings were pointed out. We have received many submissions this year of high quality, and have therefore decided that we are unable to proceed with any more surströmming. 1 / 5
