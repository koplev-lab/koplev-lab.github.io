Congratulations to our talented master student Mihkel Jesse, who has won the best poster award at this year's European Society of Human Genetics (ESHG) conference in Gothenburg. The poster presented pioneering work developing methods for "Ultra-fast genetic colocalisation across millions of traits". The work is from previous associations with Kaur Alasoo at the University of Tartu, leading to 2 recently published seminal papers in Nature and PLoS genetics.
