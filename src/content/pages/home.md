The Koplev Lab is a computational biology group in the Department of Gene Technology at KTH Royal Institute of Technology and at SciLifeLab in Stockholm, led by Simon Koplev.

We build machine learning and statistical models of cells in their tissue context, and use them on single-cell and spatial omics data from human tissue. People with backgrounds in machine learning, mathematics, computer science and statistics are as welcome in the group as people from biology and medicine.
