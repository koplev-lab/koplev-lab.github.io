We welcome PhD students, postdocs and master students with backgrounds in machine learning, mathematics, computer science, statistics, biology or medicine. Send a short email with your background, what you would like to work on, and a CV.

We are also glad to hear from potential collaborators, whether you develop methods, generate tissue data or study the diseases we work on. Write to Simon with a few lines on what you have in mind.
