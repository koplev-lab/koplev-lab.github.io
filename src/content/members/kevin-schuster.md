Kevin Schuster is an MSc student in Molecular Techniques in Life Science, a joint programme of KTH, Karolinska Institutet and Stockholm University. His work focuses on transposable elements (TEs) in the aging human brain, using single-nucleus and long-read sequencing data to study how changes in TE methylation and expression relate to inflammation. He aims to understand how TEs contribute to inflammaging and how this might relate to neurodegenerative diseases.
