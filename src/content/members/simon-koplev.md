Simon is an Assistant Professor in Computational Biology at KTH and a SciLifeLab Fellow. He holds a PhD in Medical Science and did his postdoctoral research at the University of Cambridge. He has worked in academic bioinformatics for twelve years and takes part in open science efforts such as the Human Cell Atlas.
