Bára Vavřinková is a third-year bachelor’s student in Medical and Pharmaceutical Biotechnology at IMC Krems University of Applied Sciences in Austria. For her bachelor thesis in the lab, she analyzes single-cell transcriptomic data to study the role of fucosyltransferase (FUT) genes in inflammatory bowel disease.
