Mihkel Jesse is an MSc student in Molecular Techniques in Life Science, a joint programme of KTH, Karolinska Institutet and Stockholm University. In the lab, he has worked on several single-nucleus multiome (paired RNA-seq and ATAC-seq) projects. In his current project, he maps cell-type-specific caQTLs and eQTLs in the human prefrontal cortex to assess how prominent chromatin priming is in psychiatric disease compared with diseases of other tissues. Before this, he used multiome data to study priming and gene regulatory networks in tissue-resident memory T cells, and contributed the multiome analysis for the Human Heart Cell Atlas. He completed his bachelor's in mathematics at the University of Tartu, where he worked in the Alasoo Lab and developed gpu-coloc, an ultra-fast tool for genetic colocalisation.
