Julia Nyman is a Research Engineer in the lab and holds an MSc in Medical Biotechnology from KTH. Her work focuses on intestinal spatial transcriptomics, using Xenium data to study tertiary lymphoid structures (TLS) across inflammatory diseases. She aims to uncover the mechanisms behind TLS formation and function, and how they contribute to disease. She joined the lab during her master’s thesis, where she built a spatial atlas of immune niches in the human gut.
