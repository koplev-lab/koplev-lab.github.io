Mohamad Askari is an MSc student in Machine Learning at KTH and a Research Assistant in the lab. He develops ML methods for spatial transcriptomics and single-cell omics, as well as analysis methods for mass spectrometry imaging (MSI) data. Before KTH, he completed his BSc in Computer Science and AI at Sapienza University of Rome and worked as a Research Assistant on computational biology at the Medical University of Vienna.
