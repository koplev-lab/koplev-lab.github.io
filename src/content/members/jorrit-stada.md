Jorrit Stada is a PhD student in Medical Science at Karolinska Institutet at the Schlisio lab co-supervised by Simon Koplev. He works on computational approaches and analyses to elucidate cellular plasticity and malignancy in paraganglioma and neuroblastoma. Before this position, he completed a joined MSc in Molecular Techniques in Life Science at KTH, KI, and SU and worked on micro-scale level tumour dynamics and disseminated tumour cells at the Hausser lab.
