Zihan Wang is an MSc student in Bioinformatics at Saarland University, Germany. Her current project focuses on characterizing transcription factor isoform usage across human tissues and investigating how alternative isoforms shape gene regulatory networks using single-cell and multi-omics data. Previously, she worked as a Research Assistant in the Müller Lab, using explainable machine learning to study spatial signaling in the human thymus, and as a Research Intern in the Ziegenhain Lab at Karolinska Institutet, studying alternative splicing in the human prefrontal cortex.
